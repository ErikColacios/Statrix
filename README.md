# Statrix 🎮

Statrix is a modern web platform that lets gamers **share their gaming journey**, connect with friends, and explore a growing database of video games.  

With Statrix, you can:  
- Build your **public gaming profile**.  
- Discover and explore a **comprehensive video game database**.  
- **Rate, review, and favorite** your favorite titles.  
- Create **custom game lists** to showcase your backlog or recommendations.  
- Connect with friends and see what they’re playing.  

---

## 🚀 Tech Stack

- **Next.js 14** – App router, optimized for modern React.
- **Tailwind CSS** – Utility-first styling with dark/green theme.
- **TypeScript** – Strongly typed for safety and scalability.
